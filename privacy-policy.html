Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

At allalo AI (accessible via ai.allalo.com), accessible across our home directory, blog articles, and Tools Pro applications, protecting visitor privacy is one of our main priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by allalo AI and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

allalo AI is engineered as a client-first, privacy-focused search directory. We do not require account creation or personal user registration to browse our catalog of 21,821+ AI tools.

  • Local Storage: We use browser localStorage exclusively to preserve your personal preferences (e.g., Dark/Light theme mode, starred Favorite tools, and your matrix comparison list). This data never leaves your local device.
  • Form Submissions: If you submit an AI tool via our Developer Submission form, we collect the submitted tool details and contact email solely for review and inclusion.

2. Log Files & Analytics

Like most standard web platforms, allalo AI utilizes web server log files. These logs include Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and click counts to analyze trends, administer the site, and track global system performance. None of this information is linked to personally identifiable information.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to allalo AI and/or other sites on the Internet.

  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Third-party ad networks may use technology in their respective advertisements that appear on allalo AI, sending directly to your browser. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs.

4. Proprietary Tools & Blog Processing

Inputs provided into our Tools Pro utilities or blog interactive scripts are processed locally within client browser memory or via encrypted Stateless API endpoints. We do not store, index, or harvest prompt outputs or personal text generated within Tools Pro applications.

5. GDPR & CCPA Privacy Rights

Under European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), users have the right to request access to, deletion of, or restriction of any personal data submitted via contact forms. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at jasimubd3@gmail.com.
